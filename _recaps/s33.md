The party gathered around Regill’s body in the street, as guards drew near and Ellywick began to freak out. They pulled everyone into an alley while the guards passed by, uninterested. Jadwiga and Saskarill took Regill’s body back to their house, while Contessa brought Ellywick to the Ventyrn church, where they were received and taken care of by Mother Zemi and the priests. Both pairs spent the night in an exhausted fugue.

As dawn came the next day, priests from the church arrived at the Embers’ house to retrieve Regill’s body. Saskarill and Jadwiga went with them, where the party united, and buried Regill, with his armour, weapon, ears, and orange rock. As they discussed the night’s events, tensions rose, and the party split off to pass the next days on their own terms. Celza and Perin were hired, issues with Amada were smoothed over, and Contessa left Inlarut

Saskarill visited Mordecai the hunter, and through him found a mountaineer named Alonso to journey with the group to the monastery and beyond. He had an interesting interaction with the party, but they decided to travel with him. Now, they set out for the monastery.
