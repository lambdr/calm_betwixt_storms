The party squared off with an ice demon that had overpowered the dark priest Besar Ceste. Alonso rushed into the cave, followed by the party, and engaged the fiend in battle. Saskarill spotted Besar’s staff, but an icy blast knocked Contessa and Ellywick unconscious. The bard struggled to stabilize the fallen casters while Jadwiga ran to collect the staff, expertly grappling it with her rope, while Alonso frantically held of the beast.

Jadwiga got the staff to the frozen priest, who thawed himself and weakened the demon, allowing the Alonso to whittle it down, and Contessa (revived by Jadwiga) to incinerate it. Exhausted, the group had some tea and watched the sun set from their mountain peak. Besar restored Jadwiga’s vitality and gifted the party a periapt of wisdom, before heading to sleep. The following morning, he told them of a ruined watchtower on the other side of Calaǔ, that has magic and treasure and might be inhabited by a giant.

The party descended Mt. Rynn towards Calaǔ, where they informed Berin of their inability to help little Emele. They also celebrated the Day of the Parting, remembering Regill with an arch on the outskirts of town. They moved on a little farther before resting for the night, and now prepare to reach the watchtower.
