The party looted the bandits’ bodies, recovering some coin, valuable gems, jewellery, and the ogre’s head. Ellywick buried their fallen allies, while Jadwiga and Regill interrogated the captured mage. After some fingers were broken, the mage divulged that he was expecting a pair of emerald earrings- the story given to Andrean Markovici. Regill passed judgment on the mage and the group traveled back to Inlarut. Relations with the surviving traders were strained by the battle, torture, murder, and Regill’s callousness towards the civilians. Despite their best attempts, other party members couldn’t heal this rift.

The bloody crew returned to the Jeweler’s Guild bearing the head of the Ogre Trirg. Guildmaster Virgil paid them in return for telling them about Markovici, and also told Regill about a pair of magical gauntlets. The party planned the next few days, as they aim to compete in the Grand Marshall’s Games. They registered for the games and then visited Llewellyn, soothing tensions after their previous visit, and learning more of his past prophecy.
