The Embers and Alonso set out from Inlarut, heading towards Xoenar’s Patience. Along the road, they came across a body being feasted upon by gigantic mosquito beasts. Contessa was quick with a *fireball*, incinerating the creatures before they could harm the party. The next day, the party began to climb towards the monastery in the hills, passing farms and vineyards, before finding a man collapsed on the side of the road. He was roused by Ellywick, and exhausted and dehydrated explained that his daughter had been taken by the monks. His daughter, Emele had been having nightmares, and the monks believed her to be cursed.

Alonso pledged to aid the man, and rushed up to the monastery, accosting two monks gardening. Jadwiga smoothed over the interaction before things could get out of hand, and they pulled the excited ‘knight’ to the Monastery’s doors. Deciding to be “sneaky” – which involved knocking on the door…

The Embers were greeted by Sister Analyn, who invited them in and offered them a meal and lodging, but did not answer their questions about Emele or Besar Ceste. They had a few minutes to themselves in the monastery, where Alonso’s valiant prayers to Lord Nin were rewarded, to Ellywick’s shock. When she verified the truth of his claims with a *detect magic* spell, she also noted magical auras below the temple. 

The dinner gong was rung, and most of the party joined the monks for a hearty meal, but Jadwiga feigned illness and stayed behind with Alonso in the temple. There, they found a secret passageway, which Jadwiga entered. Inside, she found peepholes and a locked iron door. The deft rogue was able to pick the lock and chose to descend into the lower section of the temple. 

There, she found cells, one containing a red clawed creature, another a beautiful elf named Shelara, and the third, the missing Emele. Seeing the distraught child and desperate elf, Jadwiga decided to free the pair. However, as she opened Shelara’s cell, the elf reached out and kissed her, drawing forth her life energy and enchanting the unsuspecting rogue.
