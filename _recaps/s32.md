The other Embers talked Regill and Ellywick out of breaking into the prison, suggesting instead that they visit the Fiend’s Pit for information and leads. The party arrived in the underground tavern, and Jadwiga took her leave to speak to Amada the Wraith. Jadwiga received the long-awaited response to her letter from an unknown individual, and exchanged gold for information about Calnescu. The Baronet has left the city, and any actions taken during the [Night of Stygian Rain] were done outside of the knowledge or control of the Hand. 

Meanwhile, Saskarill had travelled to the bar to speak with Magda, and the other three spotted a figure in the shadows; and decided to approach. Contessa and Ellywick slid into the shadow’s booth - they would later find his name to be Bloodworth - while Regill stood by, opposed by two brutes. The two casters asked for information from the shadowy figure. Bloodworth, seemingly not too chatty, asked what the party had to offer. When the pair failed to offer anything, just reiterating their desire for information, Bloodworth had one of his thugs throw Regill across the room as a show of force. Ellywick and Contessa were a bit affronted by this, and failed to offer Bloodworth a reason to entertain their request for information. He seemed uninterested in continuing the conversation, drawn to the scene developing with Regill, and so the casters withdrew.

Regill had not appreciated being tossed about, and so waved a metal rod threateningly at the offending brute. Bloodworth’s man attempted to swat the rod to the side, but could not budge the gnome’s steely grip. Regill began to attack the thug, causing several things to happen. First, the thug began to throw punches back. Second, the attention of the bar focused on ensuing brawl between an outsider and a member of the Hand. Third, some of the other patrons jumped in to help Bloodworth’s henchman, restraining Regill while the punches continued.

Saskarill saw this unfold and was told not to interfere in the brawl, lest that bring the Embers and the Hand into full-on conflict. She ran over to Bloodworth and tried to intercede in Regill’s beating. Bloodworth asked the bard what she could offer in lieu of his violent entertainment. She gambled on offering for the party to kill someone for Bloodworth. The assassin, unimpressed, waived her off, and she retreated to watch the scene play out.

Contessa and Ellywick, watching Regill near unconsciousness, decided not to wait to see what would happen once the fighter was knocked out. They plunged the tavern into fog, and called forth as Bloodworth to have Regill released, which worked, allowing Ellywick to restore Regill’s health and temporarily protect him with a sanctuary spell. Contessa blasted Bloodworth’s brute who had instigated things with Regill, killing him.

Rather than flee, Regill held his ground while the casters departed. Bloodworth himself had stepped forth but was momentarily unable to assault Regill. The cocky gnome spit in the assassin’s face and strolled out, reuniting with Ellywick and Contessa to hurry home. By the bar, Jadwiga and Saskarill watched in horror, and Amada watched with mild interest, as a furious Bloodworth stormed out of the fog, grabbed a crossbow, and followed the fleeing party members. 

Ellywick was sprinting home, had fallen behind her elven companion but drew slightly ahead of Regill. A thump causes her to turn, and she sees Regill collapse to the ground, a crossbow bolt in his neck. He holds out his ring to her as he dies, saying “A debt…” Above, a dark outline melts back into the rooftops, leaving Ellywick standing, once again, alone with Death.
