The party recovered from the Grand Marshall’s Gala and their icy escapade, and honed the skills they employed during their competition and recent quests. Regill meditated, Ellywick and Saskarill honed their martial skills, Jadwiga learned about prayer, and Contessa’s magical abilities grew.

The party’s eye turned to the road ahead of them. A consensus was reached that they would take the road to Grantia, and complete a job for Vizier Wadim vanTean along the way. But first, there were a few items of business to sort out. While Jadwiga went to make arrangements with Lord vanTean, Contessa and Saskarill interviewed candidates for the role of housekeeper/butler. Disregarding a disgruntled dude, they settled on hiring a young halfling named Perin as their butler/runner and a retired gnome named Celza to keep their house.

The party had learned that during the icefall, Delzoun prison had been damaged and Seraphim Groza (who had been awaiting execution) had escaped. Ellywick and Regill investigated, but finding the prison too heavily watched during the day, plan now to return at night.
