Unnatural hail and ice fell throughout Inlarut, with icy creatures stalking the city streets. The Embers of the Vale, at Grand Marshall van Bahz’s manor, regrouped and planned their escape. They were asked by the van Bahzs to escort two young half-elven nobles - Lady Costine and Lord Alyreas Nurhet - home amidst the chaos on the streets while the Grand Marshall, his daughter, Vizier Wadim van Tean, and the Maestryx readied the manor’s defence.

The group left the manor as any icy flurry blew through, and the Grand Marshall’s forces began to assault the ice monster. The party cautiously made their way through the city streets to escort the young Nurhet nobles to their home. Along the way, they were assailed by a pair of ice mephits as they attempted to cross the Wilamar Run. They narrowly triumphed and saw the Nurhets home.

The chaotic evening drew to a close as the group slowly stalked back to their house. The magical storm abated and the group retired, but not before Ellywick divulged that the Baronet seemed to have caused the storm somehow. Not only that, but he had slain much of her village some years back. That night, she dreamed once more of the deathly crossroads, but committed herself to following Lord Nin until justice was visited on Calnescu.
