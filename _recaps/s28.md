The party moved into their new abode and prepared themselves for the harvest festival.  Ellywick prayed while Regill meditated, Jadwiga managed to scope out some their competition aided by Contessa’s invisibility, while the wizard attempted to broaden her magical knowledge.

The first day of the Harvest Festival passed fairly uneventfully, most of the group too busy to participate in the games, food, or festivities. Jadwiga and Regill both competed in the archery competion, but lost out to a rival adventurer: Odidove of the Jovial Djinn. The Embers then faced off in a three-round gladiatorial contest.

The party first battled a trio of shocker lizards, their combined prowess and performance wowing the crowd. The first of their rivals was beaten by a pair of worgs. The group then battled an owlbear, defeating it handedly but failing to impress the crowd much. Their second set of rivals withdrew after needing to be rescued from a displacer beast.

Lastly, the Embers stood against a pair of dracotaur – monstrous lizard warriors. With aplomb, they vanquished their foes, winning the gladiatorial combats. The group enjoyed their victory by watching the joust conclude, the Baronet Sorin Calnescu triumphing after his surprise appearance. Now they prepare to celebrate their winnings at the Victor’s Banquet.
