Three fifths of the party was having a perfectly agreeable dinner at Xoenar’s Patience, with lovely fresh bread and local wine. Meanwhile, their friend Alonso milled around the temple, waiting for his roguish friend to return. When she did, she was accompanied by a beautiful elven woman. Said woman made a hasty escape out of the temple and the monastery.

The party, roused by an impatient Contessa as well as a startled high priestess, rushed to the entryway, just in time to see her shed her elven form, unfurl her fiendish wings and fly away. The party had released a succubus that was trapped in the bowels of the monastery. As a result, they had their hospitality rescinded, with Jadwiga suffering from the succubus’ charm and curse. The party was advised that if they did not reach Besar Ceste within 24 hours, the succubus’ curse might become permanent.

So, the party began to climb the mountain in the night, pushing themselves beyond their ordinary daily exertion, until they had made some progress, but fatigue became too much and the camped for a few hours. During one of their watches, a crow came to visit the party, but nothing else seemed to happen. In the early morning, the party resumed their hike, pushing upwards with haste. They defeated an avalanche-causing monster and a pack of mountain lions. While skirmishing with the mountain lions, the crow reappeared, but was injured in the fray and flew off. After their victory, the party summitted Mt. Rynn and came to a clearing where Besar Ceste had a cottage. 

As they approached the dwelling, they once again saw the crow, who surprised them by speaking. The crow informed the Embers that Besar was currently trapped in a cave by an ice demon. The crow advised the party that Ceste will aid the party in the fight if they can free him, either by reuniting him with his staff or through some other means.
