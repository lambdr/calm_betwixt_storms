The Embers awoke on the outskirts of Calaǔ, and took time to evaluate their strategic approach to combat. The group discussed positioning and Ellywick finally remembered to put her armour back on. Saskarill led the group up to the ruined tower, and Gretta scouted the area surrounding the tower’s hill. She saw that there was a reptilian humanoid guarding the tower and movement inside.

Gretta had noted a cave entrance into the hillside and the Embers snuck towards it. Inside, they found a first chamber with a metal plate and a dwarven inscription. Jadwiga translated it for the party, giving them a riddle. They discussed for a bit, but decided to push on and deal with it later. The group continued on and Jadwiga noticed that the tunnel ahead was at risk of collapse. She tried to find a safe route through, but failed, falling into a chamber below. This chamber also contained undead monsters: a bugbear, owlbear, and wyvern.

Most of the other party members jumped down into the fray, and used their past experience with undead to overcome the zombie and skeleton’s resistances. The group triumphed, albeit exhausted, and have climbed out of the pit to see what else the tunnels have to offer.
