Following the Embers’ triumph in the games, they got dressed up (except for Regill) and headed to the Grand Marshall’s manor. In the carriage, Ellywick told the party that she seemed to recognize Baronet Calnescu, but without too much further explanation. The party arrived at the manor, and entered the celebration as a mist began to trickle down from the mountains. 

The party mingled during a social appetizer course: Jadwiga spoke with Odidove of the Jovial Djinn, the victor of the archery contest about her experience and time trapped in Grantia. Regill and Ellywick stopped by a conversation with the enigmatic Valestia, who had provided the monsters and beasts for the games. Contessa and Saskarill spoke with some of the nobles present and learned that Lord Wadim vanTean might have use for adventurers. The Baronet arrived just before dinner, and the party was seated before Jadwiga could sneak out of the reception hall.

The Embers joined Lord Calnescu and Odidove at the victors’ table, and the main courses were served. Ellywick and Contessa, sat either side of the Baronet had a short and tense conversation with him, which eventually fell silent. After their feast and recognition, the party dispersed again. Calnescu briskly left the hall, going out into the gardens, with Ellywick following. Regill sought more information from Lord vanTean on his need for adventurers to deliver an item to the mountains near Xoener’s Patience. Contessa and Saskarill learned about the power of friendship (or was it how to spend friends like cheap currency for even an iota of additional power?) from Lady van Bahz and Dame Hillhorn.

Jadwiga snuck off into the inner chambers of the manor, thwarted by challenging locks, but eventually snuck into a map room. She struggled to open drawers on a desk, but as she began to appraise the maps and items about the room, hain began to plink against the window.

Ellywick had followed Lord Calnescu into a hedge maze, but was unable to hide from his gaze. As she challenged the Baronet, he revealed that he also knew her – or at least, where she was from, and laughed off some of her questions. Eventually, Ellywick left, exiting the hedge maze as thunder crashed nearby. Hail fell as she walked back to the manor. As Ellywick came in view of the main hall, great spikes of ice joined the hail, one crushing a waiting carriage, and a snowy creature standing forth.
